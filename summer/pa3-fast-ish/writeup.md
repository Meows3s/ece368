### Optimizations

I considered *many* ways to optimize this program, and, to be honest, most of the ideas made the runtime worse. From my experience, the whacky technical ideas (looking at you multithreading), usually resulted in minor or negative gains. The best optimizations are always structural/algorithmic, even though that is less 'cool' than a neat trick to speed up runtime (like compiler flags or similar).
